These are the terms of using repo-sensei while it is in early access, in plain words. Changes to
them are listed in the [changelog](/changelog); using repo-sensei after a change means you accept
it.

## The service

repo-sensei indexes code repositories you connect and lets you ask about them, trace them, draw
them, and propose fixes and changes to them. It is offered as it is, in early access: features
change, limits may change, and it can be wrong. Answers cite the lines they stand on so you can
check them; check them before you rely on them.

## Your account

You sign in with GitHub, and you are responsible for what is done with your account and the API
keys you create. Keep them to yourself; revoke a key or a session you no longer trust from your
settings.

## Your code and content

You keep every right you have in your code and in what you write here. You give repo-sensei the
permission it needs to provide the service to you: to copy and index the repositories you
connect, to send parts of them to the AI providers named in the [privacy](/privacy) page, and to
show the results to the people in your organization.

Only connect repositories you have the right to share this way. Repositories you publish on
[Explore](/explore) are shown to anyone.

## Limits

Each person and each organization has daily and monthly limits on what costs money to run
(questions, indexing, fixes). When the service as a whole reaches its own daily limit, it becomes
read-only until the next day (UTC). Limits are shown where they apply and may change.

## Fair use

Don't use repo-sensei to break the law, to attack it or others, to get around its limits, or to
read code you have no right to. Accounts that do may be suspended or removed.

## Changes to your code

repo-sensei changes code only through a fix or change someone in your organization asks for, and
only after a person with write access approves its plan. It then opens a pull request; it never
merges one. You are responsible for reviewing what you approve and what you merge.

## No warranty, and limits on liability

repo-sensei is provided without warranties of any kind, to the extent the law allows. To the
same extent, it isn't liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for lost data or profits,
from using it. While it is free in early access, its total liability to you is limited to what
you paid for it: nothing.

## Ending

You can delete your organization or your account at any time from their settings; the
[privacy](/privacy) page says what happens to the data then. repo-sensei may end the early access
or an account that breaks these terms, and will say so first where it can.
