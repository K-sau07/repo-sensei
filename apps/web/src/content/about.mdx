## Why I built it

Reading a codebase you didn't write is slow, and what AI tools say about code is hard to check.
repo-sensei indexes a repository, then answers questions with citations to the lines they come
from. It traces a feature from the screen down to the data it touches, draws diagrams checked
against the code, and works in your editor through MCP.

It's measured in the open. The [benchmarks](/benchmarks) show where it does well and where it
doesn't yet.
